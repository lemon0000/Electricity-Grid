Status: DRAFT_NONAUTHORITATIVE. This unit recomputes scientific correspondence
under an exact live GroupRoster's original group and BootstrapOwner guards.
It is the prerequisite for persisting the group provenance in a common owner;
it does not create that owner or authorize consumption of a returned dictionary.

All direct siblings must have retained independent N0 attachments. The typed
Manifest is prepared internally from the canonical ordinal and checked against
the complete roster header. Its lanes must exactly match direct classifications.
Proof-terminal and separate B6 obligations stay in the complete audit. Empty
rosters do not establish common inputs. Registry identity comes from the checked
study declaration, never from the caller or discovery of a current artifact.

For every arm the full historical adapter freshly reconstructs accepted N0 and
checks its original audit SHA and all historical numerical/source pins against
the retained local terminal. The exact-current adapter separately joins the
registry, outage/current source and exact workload/CFE. Its shortened normal
audit is compared field by field with the full historical audit's shared fields;
the two audit SHA values have different meanings and are not compared.

Across arms compare canonical N decision bytes, request/input identities, full
N before/after carry and objective locks; current decision including canonical
outage projection; exact exogenous payload and registry decision identity; and
the initial phase state and request produced under the same typed Manifest.
The initial phase state also binds the BoundaryAnchor/source-chain provenance
required by that owner; this is distinct from raw event IDs and timestamps.
Full per-arm audit, controller/claim/release/parent-child provenance and source
labels are retained separately. In particular source_action_audit_identity is
not a scientific equality key: its full action can contain raw clocks/event IDs.

Two complete rounds rebuild every arm in historical-then-exact order, including
initial state/request. The full per-arm audits and common scientific bytes must
remain equal across rounds. Roster/global correspondence is replayed before and
after rounds; live guards and implementation identity are checked around each
arm. The Manifest is independently prepared again before return. A changed
observation or unequal arm rejects the entire report. No journal is written.

The minimum direct ordinal supplies the canonical SourceBinding only after all
independent evidence matches. Each independently executed N retains its full
cost. No reuse edge or discount is produced. Public inspect returns a diagnostic
with independent_N0_scientific_correspondence_checked=true and fresh_at_return_only;
it is not an execution credential. The private guarded composition seam is for
a future persistent wrapper that must repeat these checks in its own transaction.

All common owner/producer/reuse/publication/resource/formal/native gates remain
false. The standalone check does not prove full catalog coverage, 192-hour
execution, actual RTS acceptance, or common-owner publication. Meaningful tests
must distinguish synthetic adapter/orchestration faults from actual accepted
source-root integration. Independent R3 PRE_SEAL review is required.
